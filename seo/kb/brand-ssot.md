## Core Offerings & Structural Families
- **Rigid gift boxes** (`/packaging/custom-rigid-boxes`): Magnetic book-style, two-piece lift-off lid, shoulder-neck, drawer, double-door, hexagonal/round, and fold-flat collapsible rigid boxes (typically 800–1,500 gsm greyboard wrapped in 128–157 gsm C2S art paper or 120 gsm dyed specialty stock).
- **Folding cartons** (`/packaging/folding-cartons`): Straight-tuck, reverse-tuck, auto-bottom, and sleeve-tray paperboard cartons (typically 300–400 gsm SBS ivory card or kraft board).
- **Corrugated boxes** (`/packaging/corrugated-boxes`): Roll-end front-tuck mailers (E-flute / B-flute) and export shipping outer cartons (B-flute / BC double-wall).
- **Custom paper bags** (`/packaging/custom-paper-bags`): Reinforced Euro totes and retail bags (210–300 gsm stock, 450 gsm turnover top and bottom card, knotted cotton/PP/grosgrain handles).
- **Custom fitted inserts** (`/packaging/custom-inserts`): Folded paperboard cradles, die-cut EVA foam (38–45 kg/m³), EPE/PU foam, and molded pulp trays.

## Commercial Rules & MOQ
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)**: `1,000 pieces per design`.
- **Minimum Order Value**: `US$2,000 in product value per order`, excluding freight, sample charges, tooling, and other fees.
- **Workflow**: Physical sampling before mass production (`custom-packaging-sampling-process`). Pricing, tooling, and freight are itemized separately after specification review.

## Boundaries (What MTT Does NOT Do)
- No ready-made / blank stock box retail under 1,000 pieces per design.
- No sterile-barrier medical pouches or clinical-use claims (secondary presentation & transport packaging only).
- No fabricated customer case studies, unverified lab-test passes, or blanket certification claims (FSC/PPWR/food-contact compliance requires project-specific documentation).
- Concept product images are illustrative design visualizations, not proof of delivered client orders or stock availability.
