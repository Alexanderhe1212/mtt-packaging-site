## Facts and practical guidance

The size formula is deterministic arithmetic: each product axis plus twice the selected per-side allowance. The visible 180 × 120 × 50 mm / 5 mm example gives 190 × 130 × 60 mm; it is explicitly an illustration, not a universal factory tolerance. Unit conversion uses 25.4 mm per inch. Tests independently check the resulting measurements and conversion behaviour.

Practical judgments: usable internal space is distinct from the external envelope; bag order is width × gusset × height; protection/inserts and assembly may change allowance; board, wraps and closure geometry require structural review; previews do not approve production. Information gain includes an interactive calculation, size-basis choice, a measurement checklist and a complete downloadable enquiry brief. The page makes no new claims about certificates, customers, manufacturing capacity, lead times, MOQ, prices or measured outcomes.

The bottom guidance is collapsed on user request, remains user-accessible and is server-rendered. The sole primary commercial next step is to send dimensions, structure, artwork direction, quantity and destination to Hugo for review before a formal quote.
