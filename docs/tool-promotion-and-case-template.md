2. Choose a structure and explain one tradeoff.
3. Upload an owned/authorized logo; click a layout.
4. Show the preview and saved specification.
5. End with “Send your brief for engineering review”. Do not call the preview a production dieline.

## Real-case evidence to collect before publishing
- Customer permission or approved anonymization; do not expose confidential specifications.
- Product purpose and measured dimensions, quantity and destination if shareable.
- Actual board/wrap, insert, print and finish specifications.
- Original problem and the chosen solution, including a tradeoff.
- Actual sample/production photos: closed, open, corner, insert, finish close-up.
- What was approved/tested, by whom and when; only report verified results.
- No generated image presented as a delivered product. No invented savings, certifications or testimonial.

## Weekly scorecard
Compare equal periods. Record GSC non-brand impressions/clicks by core page and target country, builder entries, quote starts, successful submissions, and manually qualified enquiries. A WhatsApp click does not prove a conversation. Consent limits analytics completeness. Keep campaign links consistent; never put customer details into event parameters or URLs.
