Used narrowly for the role of a structural identifier. It is not evidence that a specific filled package has passed a performance test. No design drawings or tables copied.

## Original packaging judgments

- Define whether the box is a consumer-facing parcel or a master carton before deciding structure or cosmetic criteria. Repacking can remove a protective layer.
- Require explicit supplier proposals for unknown fields. A blank RFQ field lets different assumptions masquerade as comparable prices.
- Separate board property measurements from the filled package's performance. Choose the investigation from the failure: wall collapse, moving product or surface marking may require different changes.
- The illustrative tall pump bottle and squat glass jar scenario tests the reasoning behind support placement. Restraining only the base can leave the upper body free to approach the adjacent jar. This is a hypothetical candidate for engineering review, not reported experience or test evidence.
- Distinguish structural sample approval, finished appearance approval, transport assessment and production release; identify substitutes and unresolved conditions in each.
- Require common quantity, trade term, named delivery point, scope and timing assumptions before comparing supplier prices. No monetary example or invented quote is used.
- Carry the revision into the purchase order and reopen affected checks after product, board, insert, closure or route changes.
