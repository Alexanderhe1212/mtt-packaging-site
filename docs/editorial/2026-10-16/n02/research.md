## Claim classes and original engineering guidance

Verified facts: single-wall layer construction; qualitative E/B profile distinction and common converter applications; separate material/package test categories; ISTA screening/simulation distinction.

Conditional engineering judgments (not claimed as MTT completed tests or customer experience):

- Diagnose the failure/contact path before choosing a deeper flute. Product motion or lid interference can remain unresolved after changing board.
- Keep required usable space independent of the flat die drawing; caliper changes affect folded buildup, locks and insert compatibility. Converter decides tool reuse, adjustment or replacement for the actual design.
- Compare the proposed print route and liner as well as flute; differences between process routes cannot safely be attributed to flute alone.
- An ECT value is not a finished-pack transit result. Compare the actual products, restraints, closures and changed components against one stated acceptance brief.
- Fix either internal or external constraints explicitly and measure the sealed sample before shipping-volume comparisons.

Information-gain types: comparison table; conditional engineering reasoning; explicitly hypothetical failure scenario; tooling/production constraints; six-point buyer brief; sampling/acceptance method; concise buyer FAQ.

No fixed caliper, safe load, universal tolerance, damage reduction, delivery time, price, production capacity, factory ownership, customer result, certification or food-contact/barrier promise. Legal/forest certification topics are not needed for this narrow intent and are not introduced. No indexing/ranking/AI-citation guarantee.
