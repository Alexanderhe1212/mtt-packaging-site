## Source and checks

One inch is exactly 25.4 mm.[1] The reference conversions are arithmetic: 100/25.4 = 3.94, 200/25.4 = 7.87, 250/25.4 = 9.84 and 300/25.4 = 11.81, rounded to two decimals. They are single-dimension examples, not recommended bag sizes. The agreed drawing unit controls; conversions are reference values, not a manufacturing tolerance.

The measurement chart is editorial specification guidance. It asks the buyer and supplier to agree reference edges and check the complete loaded sample. Existing construction-dependent load qualifications stay intact.
