# California gift packaging data for a sample comparison

Reviewed November 7, 2026 for the existing English sustainability page. The buyer’s question is which supplier figures to request when comparing two versions of a pack for California. Current Search Console demand for this wording was unavailable.

## Evidence and editorial choices

- CalRecycle published its [updated source reduction baseline](https://www2.calrecycle.ca.gov/Docs/Publications/Details/1782) on November 2 using producer-reported data. The baseline year remains 2023.
- The [program guidance](https://calrecycle.ca.gov/packaging/packaging-epr/sourcereduction/) assigns statewide targets to the producer responsibility organization. Its [reporting guide](https://www2.calrecycle.ca.gov/Docs/Web/131541) distinguishes component count from plastic-only weight in a mixed-material item.
- The component sheet, sample drawing revision and loaded fit check are procurement advice. The two 3 g inserts and single 6 g insert are an illustrative comparison. Applicability and the reporting party depend on the brand and pack.
- An independent factual review checked these claims and the arithmetic against the agency sources. Wording was revised to distinguish plastic weight from total item weight, specify plastic coatings only and include quantities offered for sale. No unresolved factual issue was identified.
