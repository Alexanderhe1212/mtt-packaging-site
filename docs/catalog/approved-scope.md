# Approved catalogue rollout

The catalogue target is approximately 500 products across four separate families. The current phase is 120 rigid gift boxes, split into six application groups of 20. Folding cartons, corrugated packaging and shopping bags follow separately. Do not populate the rigid-box collection with those structures.

## Current application groups
- Fragrance and cosmetics: bottle fit, removable inserts and gift presentation.
- Tea, coffee and chocolate: wrapped assortments, seasonal gifting and serving sequence.
- Nutrition and wellness: sealed containers, organised sets and removal access.
- Jewelry and watches: display, linings and keepsake presentation.
- Bakery and food gifting: individually wrapped contents; direct-food-contact suitability requires a separately reviewed liner and supporting documentation.
- Corporate gifts: occasion, quantity customisation and organised gift sets.

## Per-product delivery gate
1. One complete closed hero, plus open, side/rear, interior and finish-detail views.
2. A removable lid must be detached and placed beside the base with a visible gap. Never depict it as hinged.
3. Drawer, book, shoulder, door and shaped constructions retain their own opening mechanics.
4. Match outline, proportions, wrap artwork, inserts and finishes across all five views.
5. Explain structural board, wrapping paper and insert separately in English and Chinese.
6. No unsupported food-contact, drop-test, certification, pricing or delivered-client claims.
7. Record visual review before release; regenerate failed assets instead of renaming the intended structure.
8. Verify category/structure/material filtering, gallery controls, mobile layout, language parity, enquiry product ID, metadata and static output before publishing.

The earlier 100-record draft is preserved in archive/initial-100-products.json. Draft assets are not evidence of completed or deployed products.
