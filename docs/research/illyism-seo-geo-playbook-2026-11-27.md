# Illyism SEO/GEO Playbook (MTT Packaging Edition)

Integrated from `https://github.com/Illyism/seo-skills` (30 open-source SEO/GEO skills) and adapted to MTT Packaging's verified fact base (`seo/kb/brand-ssot.md`) and `docs/content-quality-pipeline.md`.

## 1. First-Hand Proof & Brand SSOT (`proof-kb`)
- Every new or upgraded guide must pull specs, MOQ (`1,000 pcs/design`, `US$2,000` product value), and boundaries strictly from `seo/kb/brand-ssot.md`.
- Open with a concrete engineering check, tolerance, or measurement mechanism in the first 2 sentences—never generic industry clichés.
- Include verifiable proof types: caliper/GSM/clearance comparison tables, failure-mode notes, sample-approval checklists, and inline links to primary standards (FEFCO, ISO 12647, Pantone, GS1).

## 2. Answer-Engine & De-Slopping Pass (`unslop-seo`)
- **First 40 words**: State the direct engineering answer immediately (suitable for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews chunking).
- **Headings**: Sentence case, logical `H1 -> H2 -> H3`, at least one buyer-question `H2` followed by a 1–2 sentence direct answer.
- **Zero AI Slop**: Eliminate empty filler (`In today's competitive market`, `elevate your brand`, `seamless`, `one-stop solution`), unattributed claims (`experts agree`), and excessive em-dashes (`—`).
- **Competitor-Swap Test**: Every paragraph must name a real packaging mechanism, material GSM/caliper, clearance mm, or MTT workflow—never generic copy that could fit any factory.

## 3. Traffic-to-Money Internal Linking (`internal-linking`)
- Informational guides (`/insights/*`) earn top-of-funnel impressions; money pages (`/packaging/*`, `/products/*`, `/tools/box-size-calculator`, `/tools/gift-box-solution-builder`, `/request-a-quote`) convert.
- Note MTT site rule: `/request-a-quote` links in guides belong in `<aside>` / CTA blocks, while body sections link naturally to `/products/*`, `/packaging/*`, `/industries/*`, and `/tools/*`.
- Never leave high-impression guides without contextual links to their matching product/category/calculator pages.

## 4. Title & CTR Optimization (`seo-title-optimization`)
- Target **40–55 characters** (strictly `< 70` chars) with primary query near the front, clear buyer benefit/constraint, and zero truncation.
- Align `seoTitle`, `summary` (≤160 chars), `H1`, and URL slug around one distinct buyer intent (no cannibalization).

## 5. Refresh, Title Audit & Honest Outreach (`blog-refresher`, `seo-roast`, `honest-link-outreach`)
- Refresh thin or stagnant guides (positions 8–20 in GSC) with updated `dateModified`, `<table>` comparisons, and `FAQPage` Q&As.
- Strictly free, engineering-led guest pitches (`info@mttpackaging.com`)—zero paid links, zero PBN spam.
