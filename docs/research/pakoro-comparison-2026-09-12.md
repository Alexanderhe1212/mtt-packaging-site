# Pakoro / MTT comparison — 2026-09-12

Scope: public homepage, gift-box landing page, company and packaging page content; MTT homepage, catalogue and detail/quote source. Browser captures were affected by repeated browser timeouts and overlapping popups. This is preliminary competitor/content research, not a complete responsive, accessibility or performance audit. Neither competitor analytics nor conversion rates are available.

Sources: https://pakoro.com/ ; https://pakoro.com/gift-box-packaging-manufacturer/ ; https://pakoro.com/packaging/ ; https://pakoro.com/company/

Findings:
1. Pakoro supplies many dedicated intent-specific category links. MTT catalogue filters are client-side and do not create dedicated category landing URLs. Add useful server-rendered industry/structure entry pages by reusing existing industry pages before creating duplicates.
2. MTT hero's abstract copy is less explicit than a direct custom luxury rigid-box proposition. Make the primary action product browsing and retain a direct quote action. The design tool should remain an optional assisted route.
3. Pakoro combines samples, payment stages, manufacturing and delivery explanation in its gift-box landing page. MTT has these across several pages. Bring concise procurement answers closer to products; only use confirmed MTT terms.
4. MTT has 120 rigid-box designs and five views each, but image concepts do not substitute for documented samples and production evidence. Add real samples, artwork-to-sample comparisons and approved anonymized cases as available.
5. MTT related products currently use the first three same-category records; improve relevance by opening structure and intended contents.
6. Preserve MTT premium visual identity; learn page organization without copying competitor photos, logos, testimonials or factory claims.
7. Pakoro is not error-free: gift-box page repeats clothing-packaging language; its pricing section describes cost types rather than actual unit prices. Avoid importing these inconsistencies or promising instant quotes without actual instant pricing.
8. Do not infer better rankings or conversion solely from design. Assess MTT organic landing-page queries and enquiry events before evaluating the redesign.

Suggested implementation order: homepage proposition/entry paths; industry pages linked to actual product sets; product purchase information; short enquiry flow with selected product preserved; real proof content; then responsive and technical SEO regression review. Keep existing product URLs and canonical/hreflang stable.

Index status at time of research: individual Search Console success confirmed for travel-wellness-organiser-stacked-drawers (previous turn) and signature-perfume-lift-off (this turn). All 240 bilingual product URLs submitted through successful product sitemap, but individual requests for the other URLs are not complete. Browser interaction timeouts interrupted further individual submissions; no Google quota error was observed.
