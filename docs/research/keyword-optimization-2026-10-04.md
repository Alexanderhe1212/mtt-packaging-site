# Cosmetic packaging keyword pilot — 2026-10-04

## Evidence and scope

Applied seo-audit and the repository content quality pipeline. Source: authenticated Bing Webmaster Tools Keyword Research, All geography/language/device filters, 12 M, 2025-10-04–2026-10-03. UI metric is Impressions, not Google monthly search volume or this site's impressions.

Observed: cosmetic packaging 519; perfume packaging 165; jewelry packaging 65. Skincare packaging, cosmetic packaging boxes and custom serum boxes report insufficient trend data. Perfume boxes also reports insufficient trend data (a partial country table exists; no total inferred). These observations do not establish growth, conversion potential, statistical significance, or that narrower phrases have zero demand.

Decision: pilot one existing cosmetics industry page using the measured cosmetic packaging topic, qualified by custom boxes and inserts. This is a relative-demand signal among tested topics, not a claim that the long-tail phrase is high-volume. Preserve other industry and product targets; no bulk replacements or duplicate article creation. Existing ranking query data was not available in this batch, so scope is deliberately limited.
