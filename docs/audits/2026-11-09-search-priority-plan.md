## Execution gates

1. Obtain current Search Console page-to-query reports and check search-result titles and snippets for the highest-impact relevant queries. Low CTR at a poor average position does not establish a title problem.
2. Reproduce actual usability, content or crawl defects and record URL, evidence, impact, change and rollback. Preserve unrelated staged work.
3. Implement only supported fixes without keyword stuffing, invented capabilities, duplicate pages, mass canonical edits or removal of normal pages. Update editorial review where content changes.
4. Run build, applicable SEO and responsive or buyer-flow checks, then deploy through main and verify production revision, canonical, robots and response. Distinguish deployment, crawl request and actual index inclusion.
5. Compare complete, same-length periods after sufficient data; report clicks, impressions, CTR, position and enquiry evidence separately. No ranking growth can be guaranteed.
